## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-octane urban athleticism and raw iron culture. It bridges hardcore gym utility with contemporary streetwear aesthetics. The visual narrative balances monolithic dark mass with sudden, laser-focused bursts of kinetic energy. It projects discipline, grit, technical precision, and unapologetic intensity.

The visual style blends **Brutalism** and **High-Contrast Modernism**:
- **Monolithic Geometry:** Solid architectural structures, crisp angles, and strict horizontal/vertical grid anchors evoke weight plates, structural steel, and tactical apparel.
- **Micro-tactile Precision:** Razor-sharp dividing lines, technical micro-labels, coordinate stamps, and subtle metallic/zinc edge-work give digital interfaces the feel of high-end performance gear.
- **High-Impact Contrast:** Total pitch blacks juxtaposed against searing electric lime provide immediate hierarchy, commanding focus during high-tempo navigation or physical training sessions.

## Colors

The palette relies on absolute darkness accented by ultra-saturated athletic frequencies.

- **Primary (`#CCFF00` - Kinetic Lime):** The core active accent. Used for critical interactive triggers, primary calls to action, active indicators, and high-priority stock alerts.
- **Secondary (`#FF3B30` - Heat Flare):** Reserved for drops, urgent telemetry, destructive actions, high-strain heart rate zones, and flash restock notifications.
- **Tertiary (`#333338` - Industrial Zinc):** Technical framing, structural division, inactive toggle states, and precision borders that define containers without washing out the deep base.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - `Base Floor`: `#0A0A0A` (Deep Obsidian - outer page backdrop and canvas floor)
  - `Surface 1`: `#121212` (Vessel Black - card surfaces, input background)
  - `Surface 2`: `#1C1C1E` (Dark Charcoal - elevated dialogs, floating overlays, drawer components)
  - `Surface 3`: `#26262A` (Forged Carbon - hover states, chip backings, tactical panels)
  - `Text High-Contrast`: `#FFFFFF` (Primary uppercase titles, pricing, crucial metrics)
  - `Text Muted`: `#8E8E93` (Specs, technical descriptors, subheaders)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy channels aggressive athletic performance and street-level industrial typography:

- **Headlines (`Anton`):** Always transformed to `uppercase`. Anton’s vertical mass and tight proportion mirror monolithic weights, bold gym banners, and raw packaging design. Line-height is strictly clamped to prevent loose horizontal breaks.
- **Body (`Geist`):** Delivers surgical, cold readability against ultra-dark surfaces. Used for product descriptions, workout logs, user account screens, and long-form editorial essays.
- **Labels & Spec Sheets (`Space Mono`):** Monospaced technical typography reserved for product specs, SKUs, inventory counts, dimension measurements, and drop timestamps. All uppercase with wide letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts follow an asymmetric, high-density editorial grid balanced by deliberate wide voids:

- **Grid Framework:** A responsive 12-column system on desktop (`gutter-desktop: 2rem`, `margin-desktop: 4rem`), reflowing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile devices (`margin: 1rem`).
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** High-density product metadata and tech callouts use compressed gaps (`space-xs` and `space-sm`), while full-bleed editorial imagery and drop headers rely on wide spatial breaks (`space-xl`).
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Mobile`: < 768px (single-column cards, full-width thumb-zone action bars, edge-to-edge product imagery).
  - `Tablet`: 768px - 1024px (2-column lookbooks, stacked specifications).
  - `Desktop`: > 1024px (asymmetric 12-column editorial layouts, sticky technical spec sidebars, modular locker grids).

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects muddy drop shadows in favor of crisp material layering, architectural borders, and back-lit kinetic luminescence:

- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation is articulated through chromatic step-ups: `#0A0A0A` (canvas base) → `#121212` (standard modules) → `#1C1C1E` (hovered/active surfaces) → `#26262A` (overlays, context popovers).
- **Structural Zinc Borders:** Cards, inputs, and modular grids maintain separation through hairline borders (`1px solid #333338`).
- **Kinetic Glow (Accent Elevation):** Critical components (active tags, primary hover states, active audio cues) project a controlled, high-intensity neon bloom:
  - `Lime Bloom`: `0 0 16px -2px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.45)`
  - `Heat Bloom`: `0 0 16px -2px rgba(255, 59, 48, 0.45)`
- **Modals & Drawers:** High-elevation layers feature a backdrop blur of `12px` over `#0A0A0A` at 80% opacity, bordered by `#333338` to preserve distinct architectural containment.

## Shapes

The interface expresses a hard-edged, technical geometry:

- **Curvature Standard:** Micro-radius level 1. Standard components feature tight 4px (`0.25rem`) corners, scaling to 8px (`0.5rem`) on modal sheets and massive hero displays.
- **Sharp Corners:** Data matrices, SKU labels, technical divider badges, and button states can selectively employ 0px radius (`rounded-none`) to preserve a raw brutalist aesthetic.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to floating status pills and real-time live drop notifications, counterbalancing hard square grids with distinct tactical geometry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic:** Bold `#CCFF00` solid fill, `#0A0A0A` text (`Space Mono` bold uppercase). Zero to 4px radius. Sharp hover transition: shifts to `#E2F952` with active neon glow (`0 0 18px rgba(204, 255, 0, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary Zinc:** Outlined in `1px solid #333338` with `#121212` background and pure `#FFFFFF` text. On hover, border turns to crisp `#FFFFFF` with `#1C1C1E` surface fill.
- **Destructive/Alert:** `#FF3B30` solid background or border with instant full-opacity contrast.

### Cards & Lookbook Tiles
- Constructed on `#121212` with a `1px solid #333338` border.
- Features technical utility markers in the corners (e.g., micro-sized `Space Mono` sizing indicators, SKU codes, or technical weight load ratings).
- On hover, borders sharply transition from `#333338` to `#CCFF00`, with images shifting from a subtle grayscale or cold desaturation to full, hyper-vibrant saturation.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Solid `#0A0A0A` inset base, bordered with `1px solid #333338`.
- Text typed in `#FFFFFF` with placeholder copy set in `#8E8E93`.
- Active focus state: border immediately illuminates to `#CCFF00` with zero blur delay. 
- Integrated monospaced status suffixes (e.g., "[KG]", "[QTY]", "[LBS]").

### Chips & Badges
- **Drop/Spec Badges:** Jet black (`#0A0A0A`) core with an outer `1px solid #CCFF00` or `#FF3B30` border. Text in matching neon hue with a faint ambient glow.
- **Filter Chips:** Flat `#1C1C1E` with subtle `#333338` borders. When active, fill switches to `#CCFF00` with inverted `#0A0A0A` text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 16x16px boxes with micro 2px radius and `1px solid #333338`.
- Checked state fills with `#CCFF00` sporting an ultra-sharp, black angled checkmark icon.

### Rugged Athletic Additions
- **Tech Spec Strip:** Full-width, single-row telemetry tape (`Space Mono`, uppercase, `#8E8E93`) displaying fabric weight (e.g., "500 GSM"), belt thickness (e.g., "13MM"), or shaker volume specs.
- **Stock Tension Bar:** Segmented brutalist progress tracker using discrete rectangular block ticks rather than smooth continuous fills, shifting from `#333338` to `#CCFF00` or `#FF3B30` for low inventory warnings.
